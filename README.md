# IdeaVault

### A Startup Idea Sharing Platform

🔗 Live Site: https://idea-valut.vercel.app/

IdeaVault is a modern startup idea sharing platform where users can share innovative business ideas, explore ideas from other creators, and engage with the community through comments and discussions. The platform focuses on idea validation, collaboration, and innovation through community interaction.

---

## Features

- Share and publish innovative startup ideas
- Community interaction system with add, edit, and delete comments
- Secure authentication with Email/Password and Google Login
- Dark and Light theme toggle support
- Fully responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices
- Search and filter startup ideas by category
- Personalized dashboard with:
- My Ideas
- My Interactions
- Profile Management
- Trending startup ideas section on homepage
- Dynamic page titles and loading states
